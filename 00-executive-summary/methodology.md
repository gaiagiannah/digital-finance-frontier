# Methodology & Scope

## Approach

This project employs a **multi-source triangulation methodology**:

1. **Primary institutional sources**: BIS publications, FSB reports, IMF Working Papers, central bank announcements, SEC/Fed disclosures
2. **Academic literature**: Peer-reviewed papers from 2020–2026 (quantum finance, blockchain security, ZK proofs, AI agents, forensics)
3. **Industry data**: On-chain analytics (Dune, Nansen, RWA.xyz), market data (CoinGecko, Messari), corporate filings
4. **Intelligence community**: DFIR reports, threat actor tracking (Chainalysis, Elliptic, TRM Labs), geopolitical analysis
5. **Research lab outputs**: University publications, industry R&D papers (JPMorgan FLARE, Fidelity FCAT, Google Quantum AI)

## Scope

| In Scope | Out of Scope |
|---|---|
| Digital assets, DeFi, tokenization, CBDCs, stablecoins | Traditional banking operations (non-tokenized) |
| AI/ML in finance and forensics | General AI (non-financial) |
| Quantum computing (threat + opportunity) | Quantum physics fundamentals |
| PQC migration and cryptographic standards | General cybersecurity (non-financial) |
| Geopolitical analysis of digital money | General international relations |
| DFINT, OSINT, blockchain forensics | Non-financial forensics |
| Global financial institutions and organizations | Individual company financials |

## Citation Standard

- Academic citations: APA 7th edition
- Institutional reports: Name, date, URL
- On-chain data: Source and timestamp
- Market data: Source, date, methodology note
