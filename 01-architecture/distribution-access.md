## The Structural Pattern

These platforms are not competing — they are occupying **different layers of a single stack**:

1. **Settlement & acceptance** → Visa, Mastercard
2. **Distribution & access** → Schwab, Fidelity, Vanguard, Coinbase
3. **Issuance & tokenization** → Kraken (xStocks), Coinbase (Tokenize), Fidelity, NYSE/DTCC
4. **Credit & capital markets** → Kraken + Maple, Coinbase (CUSHY), JPMorgan (Kinexys)
5. **Protocol & infrastructure** → Base, Polygon, Canton, Chainlink, Fireblocks, Circle

**The 2026 inflection**: Every major financial institution has now committed to at least one layer, and partnerships are explicitly cross-layer.
