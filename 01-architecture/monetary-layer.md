# Layer 1: The Monetary Foundation

## Three Competing Digital Moneys

| Form | What it is | Who issues | 2026 Status | Best at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stablecoins** | Private, fully-backed digital dollars | Circle (USDC), Tether (USDT), Fidelity Digital Dollar, GENIUS Act issuers | $315–321B; ~98% USD; GENIUS Act + MiCA in force | Open-network distribution, cross-border portability, retail payments |
| **Tokenized Deposits** | Digital representations of insured bank deposits | Commercial banks (JPMorgan, Citi, HSBC, Lloyds, UBS) | Live on Canton, Kinexys, SWIFT ledger; $100–140T annual flows projected by 2030 | Institutional settlement, preserving bank balance sheets |
| **Wholesale CBDCs** | Tokenized central bank reserves on shared DLT | Central banks (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ, BOK, SNB, BNM) | Agorá RVT (Jul 2026); mBridge $55B+; ECB Pontes pilot (late 2026) | Safe settlement anchor, atomic PvP/DvP, public trust |

**Consensus (BIS, IMF, Citi, BCG): all three coexist.** No single winner. The end-state is an interoperable stack where the appropriate instrument is selected per transaction.

## Key Monetary Infrastructure Projects

### Project Agorá (BIS/IIF)

- **Launched**: April 2024
- **Participants**: 8 central banks (Fed, BoE, BoJ, BOK, BNM, SNB, BoM, BoC) + 28 private institutions
- **Architecture**: Layered design preserving each central bank's currency autonomy while enabling interoperability
- **RVT Completed**: July 2026 — 30 cross-border transactions, 6 currencies, ~CHF 800K, 80-second average settlement
- **Next**: Scale to production

### Project mBridge

- **Participants**: PBoC, HKMA, Bank of Thailand, CBUAE, SAMA, Bank of Korea, Bank of Mongolia
- **BIS exited**: October 31, 2024
- **Phase 4 Complete**: March 2026 — $4.3B in 90 days, 98% cost reduction
- **MVP Target**: Q3 2026
- **Cumulative**: $55.5B; e-CNY = 95% of volume
- **Commercialization**: Ready (FT, June 2026); considering HK incorporation

### Project Mandala (BIS)

- **Focus**: Compliance-by-design using ZK proofs
- **Phase 2**: 8 central banks (RBA, BOK, BNM, MAS, BoF, RBI, CBK, BSP)
- **Technology**: P2P messaging + rules engine + ZK proof engine

### ECB Pontes / Appia

- **Pontes**: Connect DLT to TARGET2; pilot late 2026
- **Appia**: Long-term tokenized CBDC framework; report 2028

### SWIFT Blockchain Ledger

- **Launched**: July 9, 2026; 17 banks; Hyperledger Besu
- **Limitation**: Final settlement still via existing rails

### The Clearing House (TCH)

- **Participants**: JPMorgan, Citi, BofA, Wells Fargo, BNY, Barclays + 12 others
- **Target**: H1 2027

### Canton Network

- **Focus**: Privacy-preserving institutional DLT
- **Status**: Live; top fee-generating institutional chain Q1 2026
