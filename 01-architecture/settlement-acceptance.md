## The SWIFT Disruption

SWIFT's blockchain ledger is a **defensive move**. The big banks are building their own tokenized deposit networks (Canton, Kinexys, TCH). SWIFT gets to be *one option among many*, useful when a correspondent bank leg or customer requirement forces its inclusion. **It is no longer the default.**
