## Value Capture Analysis

| Layer | 2026 Revenue/Value | 2030 Projection | Key Value Accrual |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1: Monetary | $315B (stablecoins) + $55B (mBridge) | $500B+ stablecoins + $100-140T flows | Sovereign (central banks) + issuers (Circle, Tether) |
| L2: Protocol | $8.9B protocol revenue | $30-50B | Protocol operators (Ethereum validators, L2 sequencers) |
| L3: Settlement | $7B (Visa) + $1T target (MC) | $5-10B transaction fees | Networks (Visa, MC) + ledgers (Canton, SWIFT) |
| L4: Tokenization | $37B AUM (RWA) | $2-16T AUM | Issuers (BlackRock, JPMorgan) + platforms (DTCC, NYSE) |
| L5: Distribution | $50B+ revenue (combined) | $200B+ | Platforms (distribution = moat) |
| L6: Regulation | $0 (public good) | $0 | Institutions (regulatory capture = market access) |

## Strategic Insight

The highest value accrual in 2026 is at **Layer 5 (Distribution)** — whoever controls the client relationship captures the most. By 2030, value migrates to **Layer 4 (Tokenization/Issuance)** as the asset base grows 100x. **Layer 3 (Settlement)** is the "toll road" — lower margin but structurally essential.
