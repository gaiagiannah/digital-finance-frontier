# FSB Crypto-Asset Regulatory Framework

## Overview

- **Finalized**: July 2023 (under G20 India presidency)
- **Principle**: "Same activity, same risk, same regulation"
- **Two recommendation sets**:
  1. **CA Recommendations** — Regulation of crypto-asset service providers and markets
  2. **GSC Recommendations** — Specific rules for global stablecoin arrangements

## October 2025 Peer Review (37 Jurisdictions)

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Comprehensive CA frameworks | 11 of 37 jurisdictions |
| Comprehensive stablecoin frameworks | 5 of 37 jurisdictions |
| Gaps | Significant; creating regulatory arbitrage risk |
| FSB/IOSCO call | Enhance cross-border cooperation, reduce fragmentation |

## Key Components

### CA Recommendations
- Licensing/registration of VASPs
- AML/CFT compliance
- Consumer protection
- Market integrity
- Cross-border cooperation

### GSC (Stablecoin) Recommendations
- 100% reserve backing (safe assets)
- Redemption at par, on demand
- Reserve management standards
- Issuer resolution framework
- Cross-border coordination
- Transparency/reporting
