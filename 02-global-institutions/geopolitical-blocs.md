# Geopolitical Blocs of Digital Money

## The Structural Split

| Dimension | G7 Corridor | China/Gulf Corridor |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | Agorá, Pontes, SWIFT, TCH, Canton | mBridge |
| Currency | USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, KRW | CNY (95% of mBridge), SAR, THB |
| Regulation | GENIUS Act, MiCA, FSB framework | PBoC rules, SAMA, national frameworks |
| Technology | Permissioned (Canton, Kinexys) + public (Ethereum, Base) | Permissioned (mBridge) + state-controlled (e-CNY) |
| Philosophy | Private-led innovation + public settlement anchor | State-led digital currency + controlled innovation |
| Key Jurisdictions | US, UK, EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland, Canada | China, Saudi Arabia, Thailand, UAE, Mongolia |

## Bridge Jurisdictions

| Jurisdiction | Role | Key Initiatives |
|---|---|---|
| Singapore | Neutral hub; "translator" | MAS Project Guardian; Mandala; both Agorá and mBridge |
| UAE | Pro-innovation; Gulf gateway | VARA; SAMA integration; national blockchain strategy |
| Hong Kong | China-adjacent; mBridge commercialization | HKMA; mBridge incorporation (2026) |
| Switzerland | Crypto-friendly; banking hub | FINMA; SNB in Agorá; Crypto Valley |
| Estonia | EU digital pioneer | e-Residency; MiCA compliance |

## Sanctions & Crypto

- FATF Travel Rule applies to VASPs
- US OFAC enforcement on crypto (Tornado Cash, N. Korean exchanges)
- mBridge as potential sanctions bypass (if no correspondent banking leg)
- ZK Proofs: Prove sanctions compliance without revealing transaction details (Mandala)
