# IMF & World Bank

## IMF

### "Making Stablecoins Stable" (WP/26/74, April 2026)

- Formal model showing 100% safe-asset backing is optimal **if** central banks remunerate reserves
- **Currency substitution risk** in inflationary economies
- Calls for unified global stablecoin standards
- Warns against uncoordinated stablecoin proliferation

### Other IMF Work

- Article IV consultations increasingly include digital asset sections
- CBDC design guidance for member countries
- Financial stability monitoring of crypto markets

## World Bank

- Focused on **emerging economy risks**: currency substitution, capital flight
- Co-hosting sessions at Annual Meetings on digital finance
- Development finance implications of stablecoin growth
- Cross-border remittance cost reduction (stablecoins as channel)
