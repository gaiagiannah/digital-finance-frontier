# IOSCO & CPMI

## IOSCO

- Joint FSB/IOSCO work on cross-border stablecoin oversight
- Securities tokenization standards
- MiFID II applies to tokenized securities by default in EU
- US SEC guidance on tokenized assets ongoing
- Cross-border cooperation framework for digital asset markets

## CPMI (Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures)

- Operates under BIS umbrella
- **Recommendations on stablecoins**:
  - Reserve backing (100% safe assets)
  - Operational resilience
  - AML/CFT compliance
  - Cross-border coordination
- **Recommendations on tokenized deposits**:
  - Settlement finality
  - Netting rights
  - Legal certainty
- Works with IOSCO on intersection of payments and securities
