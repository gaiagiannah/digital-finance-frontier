# Charles Schwab

## 2026 Move

- **Schwab Crypto** launched May 13, 2026
- Spot Bitcoin and Ether for 40M+ retail accounts
- Fee: **75 bps** (undercutting Fidelity's 100 bps)
- ETF and spot coins coexist in same account, same login, same pricing

## CEO Rick Wurster Position

- Exploring stablecoins via bank consortia
- Skeptical of tokenized equities ("what problem does tokenization solve for assets that already trade for free?")
- Committed to being present if clients want it
- No staking, no stablecoin, no transfers yet — phased rollout

## Scale

$13.1T in client assets; positioning crypto as a low-friction add-on to existing portfolios.
