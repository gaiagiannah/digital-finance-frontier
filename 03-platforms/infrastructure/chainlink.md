## Key Differentiator

The **connective tissue** of the tokenized asset ecosystem. Without CCIP, tokenized assets are siloed on individual chains. CCIP enables:
- A tokenized Treasury on Ethereum to settle on Base
- A stablecoin payment on Solana to settle via Visa on Canton
- A tokenized equity on DTCC to be accessible from Kraken xStocks on Arbitrum
