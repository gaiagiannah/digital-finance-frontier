# DTCC Tokenized Securities Pilot

## Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2025 | Pilot announced; participant selection |
| July 2026 | **Production testing**: Tokenized Russell 1000 equities, ETFs, US Treasuries |
| October 2026 | **Service launch** |

## Participants

50+ firms including:
- BlackRock (BUIDL)
- Goldman Sachs (tokenized funds)
- JPMorgan
- Citi
- Various custodians, brokers, and market makers

## What's Being Tokenized

- Russell 1000 equities
- ETFs
- US Treasuries

## Significance

DTCC is the **systemically important** clearing and custody infrastructure for US securities. When DTCC goes live:
- Tokenized equities become a **regulated, institutional-grade** asset class
- T+0 settlement in stablecoins becomes standard
- 24/7 trading becomes possible
- The "tokenized vs. traditional" debate is effectively over

## Integration Points

- NYSE blockchain platform (May 2026 approval)
- Fidelity (NYSE participant)
- Kraken xStocks (Nasdaq integration)
- Chainlink CCIP (cross-chain settlement)
