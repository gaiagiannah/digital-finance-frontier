# BTCFi: Bitcoin DeFi

## Overview

Bitcoin DeFi (BTCFi) extends DeFi functionality to Bitcoin via:
- **Babylon**: Native Bitcoin staking
- **Stacks**: L1 for Bitcoin smart contracts
- **tBTC**: Trustless BTC on Ethereum

## 2026 Status

- 5–10% of BTC market cap in staking by end 2026
- Babylon: Leading BTC staking protocol
- Stacks: Growing DeFi TVL
- tBTC: Institutional BTC on Ethereum
