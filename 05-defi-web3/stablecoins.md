# Stablecoins

## Market (mid-2026)

- **$315–321B** market cap (BIS estimate, April 2026)
- ~98% USD-denominated
- Now the settlement layer for cross-border payments and corporate treasury

## Infrastructure Stack

| Layer | Players |
|---|---|
| Issuance | Circle (USDC), Tether (USDT), Fidelity Digital Dollar, GENIUS Act funds, PayPal (PYUSD) |
| Custody | Fireblocks, BitGo, Coinbase Custody, Ledger, Safe |
| Orchestration | Bridge, Crossmint, Zero Hash, Orbital, Mural Pay, MassPay |
| Payment Apps | PayPal, MetaMask, Bitso, Yellow Card, Shopify |
| Reserves | BlackRock BUIDL, Ondo Finance, GENIUS Act funds |
| Compliance | Chainalysis, Fireblocks, TRM Labs, Elliptic |

## Regulation

| Framework | Key Rules |
|---|---|
| US GENIUS Act (Jul 2025) | 1:1 safe-asset backing; banned Fed retail CBDC |
| EU MiCA (Dec 2024) | 30–60% reserves with credit institutions |
| FSB GSC Recommendations | 100% safe-asset backing; redemption at par |

## Key Risks

- Run risk (concentration in few issuers)
- Reserve opacity
- Regulatory arbitrage (issuing in lax jurisdictions)
- Currency substitution in emerging markets (IMF WP/26/74)
