# AI in DAO Governance

## Current State

- AI agents voting in DAOs (APOLLO, 2026)
- Federated learning for policy decisions (Lee et al., 2023)
- AI-generated proposals for protocol upgrades
- Natural language governance (AI summarizes proposals for voters)

## Key Research

| Paper | Contribution |
|---|---|
| "APOLLO" (Ahmed et al., 2026) | Agent participates in its own DAO governance; self-modifying policy |
| "Eight Auditability Axioms" (Phiri, 2025) | Blockchain as natural audit substrate for AI agents |
| "DAIS" (Ragupathy, 2026) | Identity layer for autonomous agents in governance |
| "Structured Outlook on Autonomous DeFi Agents" (Gularso & Wiputra, 2025) | Governance deficit as main barrier |

## Open Questions

- Can an AI agent be a "governance participant" with legal standing?
- Who is liable when an AI governance agent makes a harmful decision?
- How to prevent AI agents from forming "cartels" in DAOs?
- Regulatory: Does an AI agent need a license to "vote" in a regulated entity?
