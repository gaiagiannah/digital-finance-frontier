# Generative AI in Fintech

## Applications

| Application | Status | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance reporting | Production | Auto-generating SAR/CTR filings |
| Customer service | Production | AI chatbots at all major exchanges |
| Code generation | Production | Smart contract development |
| Research summarization | Production | Summarizing 100+ regulatory documents |
| Adversarial detection | Emerging | Detecting AI-generated phishing |
| Synthetic data generation | Research | Creating training data for AML models |
| Regulatory interpretation | Emerging | NLP for cross-jurisdictional compliance |

## Key Paper

- "Designing Generative AI Chatbots for Decentralized Finance" — Bonnet, Hannemann & Teuteberg, *Electron Commer Res* 26, 3001–3060 (2026)

## Risks

- Hallucination in regulatory advice
- Training data contamination (financial data leakage)
- Adversarial: AI-generated deepfakes for social engineering
- Regulatory: Is AI-generated compliance advice "legal advice"?
