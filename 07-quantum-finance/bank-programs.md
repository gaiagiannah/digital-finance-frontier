## JPMorgan Q-CAN (Quantum-Secured Crypto-Agile Network)

- **Deployed**: 2026
- **Function**: Crypto-agile network that can swap between classical and PQC algorithms
- **Significance**: First major bank to deploy PQC in production network infrastructure
- **Architecture**: Hybrid (classical + PQC in parallel) during transition
