# Quantum Machine Learning

## Current State

- **No production quantum ML** in finance as of 2026
- Theoretical speedups unproven at financial scale
- Hybrid quantum-classical workflows in research

## Applications

| Application | Algorithm | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud detection | QSVT, QAOA | Proof-of-concept (JPMorgan, Barclays) |
| Portfolio classification | QML | Research |
| Anomaly detection | Quantum kernel methods | Research |
| AML (graph-structured) | Quantum GNN | Theoretical |
| NLP (OSINT) | Quantum NLP | Theoretical |

## Key Challenge

Quantum ML requires:
1. Fault-tolerant QC (1000+ logical qubits) — not available until ~2030+
2. Quantum data loading (QRAM) — unsolved at scale
3. Feature engineering for quantum circuits — new discipline
4. Hybrid classical-quantum training loops — slow

## Most Likely First Production Use

**Quantum-enhanced fraud detection** at JPMorgan or Fidelity, ~2029-2030, if hardware cooperates.
