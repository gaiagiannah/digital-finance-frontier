# DFINT: Digital Forensics in FinTech

## Definition

**Digital Forensics in FinTech (DFINT)** is the discipline of investigating, attributing, and responding to cyber threats and financial crimes in the digital asset ecosystem.

## Intersections

- Traditional digital forensics (chain of custody, evidence preservation)
- Blockchain analysis (on-chain transaction tracing)
- OSINT (open-source intelligence gathering)
- AML/CFT compliance (regulatory reporting)
- Threat intelligence (actor attribution, TTPs)
