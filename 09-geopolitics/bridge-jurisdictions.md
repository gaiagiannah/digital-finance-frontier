# Bridge Jurisdictions

## Role

Bridge jurisdictions serve as "translators" between the G7 and China/Gulf corridors, enabling cross-bloc interoperability.

## Key Jurisdictions

| Jurisdiction | Role | Initiatives |
|---|---|---|
| **Singapore** | Neutral hub; both corridors | MAS Project Guardian; Mandala; Agorá + mBridge participant |
| **UAE** | Pro-innovation; Gulf gateway | VARA; SAMA integration; national blockchain strategy |
| **Hong Kong** | China-adjacent; mBridge commercialization | HKMA; mBridge incorporation (2026) |
| **Switzerland** | Crypto-friendly; banking hub | FINMA; SNB in Agorá; Crypto Valley |
| **Estonia** | EU digital pioneer | e-Residency; MiCA compliance |

## Strategic Implications

- Corporates in bridge jurisdictions can access both corridors
- Regulatory arbitrage opportunities (and risks)
- These jurisdictions are critical for global interoperability
- Competition for "digital finance hub" status is intensifying
- Singapore is the strongest "bridge" — participates in all major projects
