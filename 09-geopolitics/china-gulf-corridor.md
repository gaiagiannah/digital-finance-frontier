# China/Gulf Corridor

## Infrastructure

| Project | Function | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Project mBridge | Multi-CBDC cross-border settlement | MVP Q3 2026; $55.5B cumulative |
| e-CNY | Retail + wholesale digital RMB | ¥7T; 260M wallets; interest-bearing (Jan 2026) |
| SAMA Integration | Saudi digital riyal + mBridge | Active |
| Bank of Thailand | mBridge participant | Active |

## Philosophy

State-led digital currency with controlled innovation. Replaces correspondent banking with direct central bank money settlement. e-CNY dominant (95% of mBridge volume).

## Key Differences from G7 Corridor

| Dimension | G7 | China/Gulf |
|---|---|---|
| Money | Tokenized deposits + wCBDC | Central bank money only |
| Control | Multi-stakeholder (banks + central banks) | Central bank dominant |
| Privacy | ZK proofs (Mandala) | State surveillance capability |
| Sanctions | Subject to US/EU | Potentially outside US jurisdiction |
| Speed | Slower (multi-party consensus) | Faster (state-directed) |
| Openness | Open to public blockchain | Closed (permissioned only) |

## Strategic Implications

- mBridge bypasses correspondent banking → reduces US dollar dominance
- e-CNY interest-bearing → competitive with bank deposits
- SAMA integration → Gulf petrodollar alternative
- 95% e-CNY dominance → de facto RMB standard in corridor
- Sanctions risk: mBridge transactions may be outside US jurisdiction
