# Currency Substitution Risk

## IMF Analysis (WP/26/74, April 2026)

- Formal model showing 100% safe-asset backing is optimal **if** central banks remunerate reserves
- **Currency substitution risk** in inflationary economies: stablecoins (98% USD) could undermine local currencies
- Calls for unified global stablecoin standards
- Warns against uncoordinated stablecoin proliferation

## Affected Economies

| Region | Risk | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Latin America | High | USDC/USDT as de facto dollar; capital flight |
| Sub-Saharan Africa | High | Stablecoin remittances bypass local currency |
| Southeast Asia | Medium | e-CNY via mBridge; USDC for trade |
| Eastern Europe | Medium | Post-Russia sanctions; USD stablecoins |
| Middle East | Low-Medium | Petrodollar still dominant; SAMA integration |

## Policy Responses

| Response | Who | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ban stablecoins | El Salvador (initially), Nigeria (initially) | Reversed/relaxed |
| Regulate stablecoins | EU (MiCA), US (GENIUS) | In force |
| Issue competing CBDC | PBoC (e-CNY), others | Active |
| Capital controls | Multiple | Mixed effectiveness |
| Remunerate reserves | IMF recommendation | Partial |
