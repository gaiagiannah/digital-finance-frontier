# Regulatory Fragmentation

## Current State (2026)

| Jurisdiction | Framework | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|
| US | GENIUS Act + CLARITY (pending) | Stablecoin-focused; bans retail CBDC |
| EU | MiCA | Comprehensive; 30-60% reserve requirement |
| Japan | JFSA | Exchange-centric; strict KYC |
| Singapore | MAS | Sandbox-based; innovation-friendly |
| UAE | VARA | Pro-innovation; separate crypto regulator |
| China | PBoC rules | State-controlled; bans private crypto |
| Switzerland | FINMA | Banking-law approach; crypto-friendly |
| UK | FCA | MiCA-equivalent; stablecoin rules |
| India | RBI | CBDC (e-rupee) focused; crypto taxed |
| South Korea | FSC | Exchange licensing; staking rules |

## Key Fragmentation Points

| Issue | US Position | EU Position | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retail CBDC | Banned (GENIUS) | Digital Euro in development | Split market |
| Stablecoin reserves | 100% safe assets | 30-60% with credit institutions | Different risk profiles |
| Tokenized securities | SEC (pending) | MiFID II applies | Different compliance |
| DeFi | Unregulated (mostly) | MiCA covers some | Regulatory arbitrage |
| Mining | Legal (varies by state) | Legal (energy concerns) | Different energy profiles |

## Convergence Path

- **2026-2027**: GENIUS + MiCA + JFSA create "triangular" perimeter
- **2028-2029**: Cross-border stablecoin standards (FSB)
- **2030+**: Full interoperability (if politics allows)
- **Risk**: Permanent fragmentation if US-China competition intensifies
