# AI-Quantum Hybrid Systems

## The "AQF" Trifecta

AI + Quantum + Finance = the most complex intersection in the project.

## Current State (2026)

- No production AI-quantum hybrid in finance
- Research at JPMorgan FLARE, Fidelity FCAT, Google DeepMind
- Hardware not yet sufficient (need 1000+ logical qubits)

|---|---|---|
