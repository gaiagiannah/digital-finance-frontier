# Programmable Money

## Concepts

| Concept | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Smart Contract CBDCs | Conditional payments, auto-tax, expiration on central bank money | Theoretical; BIS research; PBoC e-CNY pilots |
| Programmable Compliance | Regulatory logic embedded in payment protocol | Mandala Phase 2 |
| Autonomous Treasury Management | AI agents executing cash management on-chain | Emerging (Coinbase AgentKit) |
| Self-Executing Contracts | Trade finance, supply chain, insurance on-chain | Pilots (mBridge, Canton) |

## Implications

| Stakeholder | Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Consumers | Faster, cheaper payments | Loss of privacy; conditional money |
| Governments | Automated tax, sanctions, welfare | Surveillance state; loss of monetary sovereignty |
| Businesses | Self-executing contracts; automated treasury | Counterparty risk in smart contracts |
| Central Banks | Direct monetary policy transmission | Bypass of commercial banks |
| Regulators | Real-time compliance | Complexity; opacity |

## The Privacy Tension

Programmable money + full transparency = financial surveillance.
Programmable money + ZK proofs = privacy-preserving compliance (Mandala vision).
Programmable money + no privacy = dystopian (Orwellian).

**The ZK proof is the critical enabler** that makes programmable money politically acceptable.
