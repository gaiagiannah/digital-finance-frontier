## The Privacy-Compliance Bridge

ZK proofs solve the fundamental tension: regulators need to verify compliance, but full transparency enables surveillance and reduces privacy. ZK proofs allow:
- "I comply with sanctions" → verified, without revealing the transaction
- "My reserves are sufficient" → verified, without revealing the balance
- "This user is over 18" → verified, without revealing the DOB
