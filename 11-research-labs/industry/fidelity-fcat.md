# Fidelity FCAT

## Center for Applied Technology (since 2019)

| Focus Area | Key Work | Status |
|---|---|---|
| VQE | Bond pricing, index replication | Experiments on Rigetti Aspen-M, IonQ |
| QAOA | Index replication | 30-50 stock universes |
| Quantum ML | Classification, anomaly detection | Research |
| Fidelity Digital Dollar | Stablecoin | Live |
| Tokenized products | First tokenized product (Sep 2025) | Live |

## Key Findings

- VQE for bond pricing: Promising but requires fault-tolerant QC for production
- Index replication: 30-50 stocks feasible on current hardware
- Quantum ML: No production advantage yet; theoretical speedups unproven at financial scale
