# Scenarios for 2030

## Scenario 1: Coordinated Public-Anchored (BIS/IMF preferred)

- Tokenized infrastructure built around safe settlement assets (wCBDC, sCBDC)
- Permissioned shared ledgers under clear governance
- Public sector retains trust anchor
- Efficiency gains without stability risk
- **Probability**: Medium

## Scenario 2: Private-Led Convergence (US model)

- Regulated stablecoins + tokenized deposits dominate
- CBDCs limited to wholesale settlement
- GENIUS Act model exported
- Private innovation drives; regulation follows
- **Probability**: **High** (current trajectory)

## Scenario 3: Bloc Fragmentation

- G7 corridor (Agorá/Pontes/SWIFT) vs. China-Gulf corridor (mBridge)
- "Bridge" jurisdictions translate
- Corporates face multiple rails; compliance surface expands
- **Probability**: Medium-High (already emerging)

## Most Likely: Hybrid of Scenarios 2 and 3

Private-led innovation within a fragmented-but-interoperable global system, with the public sector providing settlement anchors (wCBDC) and regulatory perimeters.
