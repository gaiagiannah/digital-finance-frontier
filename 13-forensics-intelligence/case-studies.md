# Case Studies

## Binance Hack (2022) — $600M

- **Actor**: Lazarus Group (North Korea)
- **Method**: Social engineering + internal access; cross-chain bridge exploit
- **Forensic approach**: On-chain tracing identified ~$300M moved to known Lazarus wallets; OFAC sanctions; FBI involvement
- **Key lesson**: Cross-chain bridges are a primary attack vector; insider threat remains critical

## FTX Collapse (2022)

- **Actor**: Insider (Sam Bankman-Fried)
- **Method**: Misappropriation of client funds; accounting fraud
- **Forensic approach**: On-chain tracing of client deposits; KYC data from exchanges; legal process for off-chain records
- **Key lesson**: Centralized custody creates single points of failure; on-chain transparency can expose fraud

## Ronin Bridge (2022) — $625M

- **Actor**: Lazarus Group
- **Method**: Compromised validator keys (5 of 9)
- **Forensic approach**: On-chain tracing; wallet clustering; dark web monitoring for fund movement
- **Key lesson**: Multi-sig security; validator key management

## Wormhole Exploit (2022) — $326M

- **Actor**: Unknown
- **Method**: Forged token bridge message (signature verification bypass)
- **Forensic approach**: Smart contract analysis; message tracing; fund tracking across chains
- **Key lesson**: Cross-chain message verification; bridge security audits

## Poly Network (2021) — $611M (Recovered)

- **Actor**: Unknown (later identified as "White Hat" hacker)
- **Method**: Exploited validator key management
- **Forensic approach**: On-chain tracing; negotiation; full recovery
- **Key lesson**: Response speed + negotiation can recover assets; on-chain transparency aids recovery
